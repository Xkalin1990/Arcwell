Privacy Policy

App: Arcwell  |  Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arcwell (the "App") handles information entered into it. Please read it carefully before using the App.

1. Who this policy applies to

Arcwell is a professional tool designed for independent personal trainers and fitness coaches aged 18 and over. It is not intended for use by or directed at children under the age of 18.

2. What Arcwell does

Arcwell allows personal trainers to:

  • Record client intake information (contact details, health history, fitness goals, baseline measurements and consent).
  • Track client progress over time (weight, measurements, check-ins and session notes).
  • Generate professional PDF reports and export data to CSV.
  • Back up and restore client records.

3. Data collection — the short version

Arcwell does not collect, transmit, or store any data on external servers. All information you enter into the App is stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to your data or your clients' data at any time.

4. Data stored locally on your device

The following types of information may be entered and stored locally in the App:

  • Client personal details (name, contact information, date of birth, address, occupation).
  • Health and fitness information (PAR-Q screening answers, medical history, medications, injuries, fitness goals and lifestyle data).
  • Baseline and progress measurements (weight, body measurements, heart rate, blood pressure).
  • Progress photos saved to your device's local storage.
  • Consent records and client signatures.
  • Session notes and check-in logs.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it yourself (for example, by sharing a PDF with a client or saving a backup file to your own storage).

5. No accounts, no cloud, no analytics

Arcwell does not require you to create an account. There is no cloud storage, no user login, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting service integrated into the App. We do not receive diagnostic or usage data from your device.

6. Camera access

The App requests permission to access your device camera solely to take client profile photos and progress check-in photos. Photos are saved directly to your device and are not transmitted anywhere by the App.

7. In-app purchases and subscriptions

Arcwell offers an optional Pro subscription through Google Play. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive, store, or process your payment card details or billing information. For information about how Google handles payment data, please refer to Google's own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Your responsibilities as a trainer

When you use Arcwell to record information about your clients, you are acting as the data controller for that information under applicable data protection law (including the UK GDPR where relevant). This means you are responsible for:

  • Obtaining appropriate consent from your clients before recording their personal and health information.
  • Keeping your device secure and protecting client data from unauthorised access.
  • Complying with applicable data protection laws in your jurisdiction.
  • Retaining and deleting client records in line with your legal obligations.

Arcwell provides the tools; you are responsible for how you use them in relation to your clients.

9. Data security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, the security of that data depends on the security of your device. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping your operating system up to date. If your device is lost or stolen, the client records stored in the App may be accessible to anyone who can access your device.

We recommend using the App's built-in backup feature regularly and storing backup files securely.

10. Data retention and deletion

You are in full control of your data. You can delete individual client records within the App at any time. Uninstalling the App will remove locally stored data. Backup files saved to your device storage should be deleted manually if no longer needed.

11. Children's privacy

Arcwell is intended for use by adults aged 18 and over. The App is not directed at children. If a trainer uses the App to record information about a client who is under 18, appropriate parental or guardian consent should be obtained by the trainer prior to doing so. We do not knowingly collect any information about children through the App.

12. Third-party services

The only third-party service integrated into Arcwell is Google Play Billing, used to process Pro subscription payments. No other third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks or data-sharing integrations are present in the App.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
